# The d-bar distance between invariant determinantal measures is bounded by the trace norm

Yitzchak Shmalo, Einstein Institute of Mathematics, The Hebrew University of Jerusalem, Jerusalem, Israel.

Let Γ be a finitely generated sofic group. Lyons and Thom bounded Ornstein's d-bar distance between the determinantal
measures P_Q and P_Q' of two equivariant positive contractions on ℓ²(Γ) by a function of the operator norm of Q − Q'
and by a power of its trace norm. The bound by the trace norm itself is known when Q and Q' commute, and Lyons and
Thom asked whether it holds in general. We show that d-bar(P_Q, P_Q') ≤ ‖Q − Q'‖₁ for all such Q and Q', and for equivariant
positive contractions on the edges of a Cayley diagram, with equality when Q ≤ Q'. The proof joins Q to Q' by short steps. Each step
subtracts a small multiple of the negative part of Q' − Q and then adds the same multiple of its positive part.
By the invariant monotone coupling of Lyons and Thom, the first changes the d-bar distance by at most the trace of
the operator subtracted and the second by at most the trace of the operator added. The same argument proves the corresponding inequality for matrices on a finite set, which
Lyons and Thom had observed numerically.

The question is the one Lyons and Thom ask after the proof of Lemma 7.8 in "Invariant coupling of determinantal
measures on sofic groups", Ergodic Theory Dynam. Systems 36 (2016), 574-607 (arXiv:1402.0969), and it is repeated in
Section 5.2 of Lyons, "Determinantal probability: basic properties and conjectures", ICM 2014 (arXiv:1406.2707).
